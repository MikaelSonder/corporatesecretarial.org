Executive Summary

Corporate secretarial in China is the structured function through which a company maintains its formal legal identity, governance order and statutory administrative discipline over time. In practical terms, it is not limited to incorporation, because the operating task continues through shareholder and director administration, corporate documentation, enterprise registration, annual report publicity and the handling of changes affecting the company’s formal position.

In China, this function is closely connected to the enterprise registration system administered by the State Administration for Market Regulation, known as SAMR, and local Administrations for Market Regulation. The discipline includes maintaining articles of association and governance documents, preparing corporate decisions, coordinating changes for registration and ensuring that corporate acts are accurately reflected in the company’s formal records and enterprise-registration position.

The Chinese environment places importance on registered company particulars, legal representative authority, company seals, documentary completeness and annual information publicity through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. Corporate secretarial work therefore acts as the bridge between shareholder decisions, directors, supervisors, legal representatives, ownership information, annual reporting, legal advisers and market regulation authorities.

Cross-border relevance is substantial because Chinese entities are frequently used by foreign investors as operating subsidiaries, regional production companies and market-entry vehicles. In such cases, Chinese legal maintenance must be aligned with group approval chains, Chinese-language documentation, local registration and seal practices and multinational compliance expectations.

Purpose

The purpose of the corporate secretarial function is to preserve the legal and administrative integrity of a company in China throughout its lifecycle.

It exists to ensure that the entity's formal record, governance acts, enterprise registrations, legal representative authority, annual report publicity, beneficial ownership information and decision trail remain coherent, timely and defensible for management, owners, counterparties, regulators and auditors.

Primary Outcome

A company in China whose articles of association, governance records, corporate approvals, enterprise registration data, annual report information, beneficial ownership information and formal maintenance requirements are kept current, accurate and aligned with its actual legal and operational position.

Country Characteristics

Operational CultureChinese company administration is document-driven, registration-focused and procedurally formal, particularly where company acts require market regulation registration, updated business licence information or clear evidence of corporate authority.

1. Entity MappingIdentify the Chinese entity type, Unified Social Credit Code, business licence details, registered address, legal representative, governance structure, ownership profile and current market regulation registration position.
2. Record ReviewCheck articles of association, shareholder and director arrangements, legal representative authority, company seals, previous decisions, annual report position, beneficial ownership information and registration status.
3. Trigger IdentificationDetermine which event has activated the work, such as incorporation, annual report publicity, legal representative change, director or supervisor change, capital event, address change, business scope change, restructuring or group instruction.
4. Governance DocumentationPrepare or organise shareholder decisions, board decisions, resolutions, amended articles, approvals, powers, seal-related records or other internal governance materials.
5. Statutory CoordinationAssess whether any change requires market regulation registration, business licence update, annual report publicity, beneficial ownership information filing, tax coordination, calendar action or external authority interaction.
6. Filing and Record UpdateSubmit relevant registrations or filings where required and ensure internal company records, seals, authority records and enterprise information reflect the approved and registered position.
7. Maintenance and Audit ReadinessMaintain records, preserve decision trails, monitor deadlines and keep the entity ready for banking, audit, due diligence, regulatory review or investor scrutiny.

Is SAMR Central to Corporate Secretarial Administration in China?Yes. The State Administration for Market Regulation and local market regulation administrations are central to enterprise registration, registered changes and the enterprise information publicity system.
Do Foreign-Owned Companies in China Need Local Corporate Secretarial Maintenance?Yes. Foreign-owned Chinese entities commonly need local governance maintenance, market regulation registration coordination, corporate record control and calendar discipline.
