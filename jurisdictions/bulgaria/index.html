Executive Summary

Corporate secretarial in Bulgaria is the structured function through which a company maintains its formal legal identity, governance order and statutory administrative discipline over time. In practical terms, it is not limited to incorporation, because the operating task continues through manager and shareholder administration, corporate documentation, Commercial Register maintenance, annual financial statement publication and the handling of changes affecting the company’s formal position.

In Bulgaria, this function is closely connected to the Commercial Register and Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities, maintained by the Registry Agency under the Ministry of Justice. The discipline includes maintaining constitutional and governance documents, preparing resolutions, coordinating company changes for registration and ensuring that corporate acts are accurately reflected in the company’s formal records and public registry position.

The Bulgarian environment places importance on electronic registration, documentary completeness and public disclosure. Corporate secretarial work therefore acts as the bridge between shareholder decisions, managers, ownership information, annual reporting, legal advisers and the public registration system. Beneficial ownership information is recorded within the entity’s electronic file in the Registry Agency environment.

Cross-border relevance is substantial because Bulgarian entities are frequently part of regional operating models, foreign-owned groups and international investment structures. In such cases, Bulgarian legal maintenance must be aligned with group approval chains, Bulgarian-language documentation, local registry requirements and multinational compliance expectations.

The purpose of the corporate secretarial function is to preserve the legal and administrative integrity of a company in Bulgaria throughout its lifecycle.
